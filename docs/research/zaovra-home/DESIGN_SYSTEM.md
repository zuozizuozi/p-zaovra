# Zaovra Home Design System

## Reference hierarchy

- The frozen Zaovra information architecture still carries the earlier structural research.
- AuthKit is now the sole visual reference: midnight canvas, ice-gradient display type, blueprint atmosphere, frosted inset edges, luminous glass product surfaces, and restrained ambient movement.
- Zaovra product semantics remain authoritative: desktop workspace, BYOK, reviewable changes, and WorkGraph preview.

No third-party brand asset, copy, logo, or distinctive illustration is reused.

## Tokens

- Page background: `#05060f`.
- Deep glass: `rgba(5, 6, 15, 0.97)`.
- Frosted surface: `rgba(186, 214, 247, 0.03)`.
- Strong frosted surface: `rgba(199, 211, 234, 0.06)`.
- Heading: `#d8ecf8`.
- Primary copy: `#d1e4fa`.
- Secondary copy: `#c7d3ea`.
- Muted copy: `#9da7ba`.
- Ice accent: `#98c0ef`.
- Success status: `#79d8bc`.
- Hairline: `rgba(186, 215, 247, 0.12)`.
- Strong hairline: `rgba(186, 214, 247, 0.24)`.
- Display gradient: `linear-gradient(180deg, #d8ecf8 0%, #98c0ef 100%)`.
- Product elevation: `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(216,236,248,.20), inset 0 24px 48px rgba(168,216,245,.06), 0 24px 64px rgba(0,0,0,.42)`.

## Typography and layout

- Display: `Space Grotesk` fallback over the existing application sans stack, `clamp(54px, 6.3vw, 88px)`/0.98, weight 500.
- Section heading: `clamp(38px, 4vw, 52px)`/1.08, weight 500.
- Body: 18px/1.65 desktop, 16px/1.6 mobile.
- Product chrome and eyebrow labels: existing mono stack, uppercase, 0.1em tracking.
- Content max width: 1200px; horizontal padding 28px desktop, 20px mobile.
- Section spacing: 100–120px desktop, 40–72px mobile.
- Cards and product windows: 16px radius. Buttons: pill radius.

## Visual constraints

- Use one dominant panel per section; avoid card grids that pretend every capability is equally important.
- No hard-coded usage statistics or customer logos.
- Do not use a white/light-themed clone. Preserve Zaovra's desktop and code identity inside the AuthKit-derived visual system.
- Elevation comes from inset frost highlights and dark halos, never thick solid borders or conventional SaaS shadows.
- The blueprint grid and spotlight are atmospheric layers; they must not reduce text or product-panel legibility.
